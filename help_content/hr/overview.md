The Human Resources module tracks your own tenant's staff — separate
from Contacts (outside people) and Suppliers (vendors).

## What's on an employee record

- **Core details** — name, gender, job role, and the usual contact-info
  fields (phones, emails, addresses).
- **Manager** — each employee can report to another employee, which
  builds up an org-chart-style reporting structure across the module.
- **Job Role** — drawn from a Table Maintenance-managed lookup, so titles
  stay consistent across the tenant.

## Deleting an employee

Removing a phone, email, or address from an employee record deletes just
that entry — the employee record itself is unaffected. Deleting the
employee record is a soft delete, same as the rest of the app.
