Contacts are individuals — anyone you deal with as a person, whether or
not they're tied to an Organization or Supplier. A contact can stand
alone, or be linked to one or more Organizations/Suppliers as a point of
contact there.

## What's on a contact record

- **Core details** — name, file-as name (how they sort/search), job
  title, and notes.
- **Phones and emails** — any number of each, one flagged primary.
- **Addresses** — full street/city/region/country, using the same
  cascading Region → Country → Province/State → City pickers used
  throughout TMS.
- **Reference Links** — a simple list of URL/document-path + description
  rows, for a contact's LinkedIn profile, a shared document, or anything
  else worth a quick link.

## Finding a contact

The list page supports search by name and filters by the usual fields.
Deleting a contact is a soft delete — it's hidden from lists and searches
but not permanently destroyed.
