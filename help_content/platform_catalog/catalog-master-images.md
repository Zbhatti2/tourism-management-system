Each platform **Accommodation** and **Restaurant** has a **Master Image
Catalog**, in the same way as Points of Interest. Tenants' hotels and
restaurants that came from the platform catalog inherit these images.

## Adding images

- **Master Image Catalog** on a property's or restaurant's page opens its
  album. From there, **Add images** uploads photos, or you can ask the AI
  to find some. **View Images** opens the album to browse only.
- **Import Images** on the Accommodation or Restaurants list takes a Zip of
  photos named after the property, in the same way as POI images. It also
  runs the **AI Image Collector** for several properties at once: it looks
  on each property's own website and tourism-board pages. This is a
  platform cost.
- Give each photo a **Licence / credit**, and check it before approving
  the photo.

## What tenants get

- When a tenant adds a hotel or restaurant from the platform catalog, or
  links one, it gets every master image in its **Images Catalog**.
- After that, the tenant's **Platform images** setting (System Management)
  decides what happens when you add, re-caption or remove a master image.
  The tenant can have changes applied automatically, review each change on
  the **Image updates** screen, which is the default, or get no updates at
  all.
- Tenants can delete inherited photos they don't like, add their own,
  re-caption and reorder them. A photo a tenant deleted is never offered
  again, and a caption a tenant changed is kept.
