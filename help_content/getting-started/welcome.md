The Tourism Management System (TMS) is where Heritage Tours keeps the
people, places, and inventory a tour is built from — Contacts,
Organizations, Suppliers, Points of Interest, Services, Products, and the
Package templates assembled from all of them — plus the Human Resources,
Accounting, and system-administration tools that support running the
business day to day.

## Finding your way around

The left sidebar covers the whole app:

- **Dashboard** — your landing page, with quick tiles into Points of
  Interest and a few not-yet-built areas, plus a metrics summary.
- **Contacts** and **Organizations** — the people and companies you deal
  with, independent of whether they're also a Supplier.
- **Suppliers** — hotels, transport, guides, and every other vendor a tour
  is built from, each with its own Documents, Links and Images sub-module
  for licenses, permits, agreements, and photos.
- **Human Resources** — employee records, roles, and org structure.
- **Inventory Management** — Services and Products, the two catalogs a
  Package draws line items from.
- **Package Management** — tour package templates assembled from Services,
  Products, Suppliers, and Points of Interest.
- **Accounting / Finance** — Billing & A/R, Purchasing & A/P, and Accounts
  & G/L (early-stage placeholders today).
- **Organization Intelligence** — Documents (a tenant-wide knowledge base
  of files, links, and notes) and Intelligence (a running journal).
- **Tables & Utilities** — Data Exchange (CSV import/export) and Table
  Maintenance (the lookup tables — Supplier Types, Document Types, Amenity
  Options, and the rest — that shape dropdowns throughout the app).
- **System Management** — users, tenant settings, and administrative
  tools.

## Using this help panel

Click **Help** in the top-right corner from almost anywhere to open this
panel, already scoped to whatever module you're looking at. You can
always:

- **Browse** the full tree on the left — click a section to expand it.
- **Search** using the box above the tree — it matches page titles and
  keywords as you type.
- Keep working with the panel open — it's a drawer, not a dialog, so
  whatever you were doing underneath stays exactly as you left it.
- Press **?** anywhere outside a text field to open it without reaching
  for the mouse.
