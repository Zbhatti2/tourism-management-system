The Dashboard is what you land on after signing in. It's a starting
point, not a module of its own — nothing here is edited directly.

## The tiles

The big tiles are quick links into the areas of the app that work well as
a "go here first" jumping-off point — **Points of Interest** is fully
built; the others (Product Design, Schedules, Custom) are placeholders for
future phases and currently just say so if you click them.

## The metrics bar

A quick read on overall record counts across your tenant's data. It's a
summary, not a report — for anything you need to filter, sort, or export,
go to that module's own list page (Contacts, Organizations, Suppliers,
and so on) instead.

## Everything else lives in the sidebar

Every real module — Contacts, Organizations, Suppliers, Human Resources,
Inventory Management, Package Management, Accounting, Organization
Intelligence, Tables & Utilities, and System Management — is one click
away in the left-hand navigation, whether or not it also has a Dashboard
tile.
