Products is the second catalog under **Inventory Management**, alongside
Services — physical goods and packaged items a Package can include,
rather than a performed service.

## Pattern

Products follows the same GLOBAL-taxonomy-plus-tenant-scoped-catalog
shape as Services: a shared classification list underneath, and each
tenant's own catalog entries on top of it. If you're familiar with how
Services works, Products behaves the same way.
