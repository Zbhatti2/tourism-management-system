**AI Agents** lists every AI agent your organisation has, wherever it is
started from. Some agents are also started from other screens (the Image
Collector from a supplier's image album), but they all appear here, so you
can see what you have and what each one is used for.

## Your agents

Each card shows what the agent does, what it reads, what it gives you, how
many times it has run, and what it has cost this month. A yellow badge
shows how much is waiting for you to review. The button on the card starts
the agent:

- **Tour Planner**: plans a group tour with you, stage by stage.
- **Hotel Intelligence**: reads links or images you give it about a hotel
  and proposes updates to its details, for the Review Queue.
- **Points of Interest Intelligence**: the same for a Point of Interest.
- **Image Collector**: finds photos of your hotels, resorts and restaurants
  for you to keep or drop.

**Planned** shows agents that are on the way.

## Recent runs

One list of the latest runs of every agent, newest first. Click a row to
open the run (or, for the Tour Planner, the plan at that stage). A run with
nothing to review can be deleted with the bin button.

Agents never write into TMS on their own: what they find waits for a person
to approve, in the **Review Queue**, the image album or the Tour Planner.
The **AI Usage** button (Tenant Admins) shows what the agents have cost.
