Every Supplier belongs to a **Supplier Group**:

| Group | For |
|---|---|
| **Accommodation** | Hotels, Resorts, guest houses |
| **F&B** | Restaurants, fast food, cafés, catering |
| **Transport** | Airlines, car rental, bus services, trains |
| **External Resources** | Agents, helpers, consultants, guides |
| **All Others** | Every other supplier |

## Working a group at a time

**Suppliers** in the left-hand menu opens a list of the groups. Pick one,
say **Accommodation**, and the list shows only Hotels and Resorts. The
tabs above the list switch groups too, and show how many suppliers each
group has.

The list remembers where you are. After you edit or delete a supplier you
come back to the same list, with the same search and filters, and the
supplier you just changed is highlighted. **Back** on a supplier's page
does the same.

**New Supplier** offers only the Supplier Types of the group you're in.
Choose **All Suppliers** to see every Type, grouped.

## Which group a supplier is in

A supplier's group comes from its **Supplier Type**. When you change its
Type, its group follows. An External Resource is always in External
Resources.

To change which group a Type belongs to, go to **Tables & Utilities →
Table Maintenance → Supplier Types**, edit the Type and choose its
**Supplier Group**. Every supplier of that Type moves with it. New Types
get a group suggested from their name, for example "Car Rental" goes to
Transport. Check it when you add one.
