Suppliers are the vendors a tour is built from — hotels, transport
companies, guides, restaurants, and any other business you book through.
Every Supplier has a **Supplier Type** (Hotel, Transport, Guide, ...) and
optionally a more specific Subtype.

## What's on a supplier record

- **Core details** — supplier name, type/subtype, and notes.
- **Addresses, phones, emails, and Reference Links** — the same
  contact-info shape used across the app.
- **Contacts** — people at this supplier, each with their own phones and
  emails.
- **Hotel-specific sections** — when the Supplier Type template is
  "Hotel," the form grows an **Amenities & Facilities** checklist and a
  **Room Types** list, both drawn from Table Maintenance-managed lookups
  so the options stay consistent across every hotel.
- **Documents, Links and Images** — see that page in this section for the
  full writeup; it's the sub-module for a supplier's licenses, permits,
  agreements, and photographs.

## External Resources

A Supplier can be flagged as an **External Resource** — a lighter-weight
record (often bulk-imported) representing a vendor you haven't fully
onboarded yet. External Resources show up in Suppliers lists and filters
alongside ordinary suppliers, just with fewer fields filled in.

## Finding a supplier

The list page's filters cover Supplier Type, Subtype, and the usual text
search. The **Docs** column shows how many Documents, Links and Images
entries each supplier has on file.
