Every Hotel, Resort and Restaurant has an **Images Catalog**: a curated
photo album you can browse as a slider or as a list. Open it with the
**Images Catalog** button on the supplier's page.

## Adding photos

**For one hotel or restaurant:** on its page click **Add images**. Choose
one or more photos, or a Zip of them. Any file names are fine.

**For many at once:** go to **Data Exchange → Import Hotel & Restaurant
Images (Zip)**. Put the photos in a folder, Zip it and upload it. Photos
can arrive by WhatsApp, email or anything else; only the file names
matter:

- **Each file name must start with the exact Hotel / Restaurant name,
  then a hyphen.** For example `Hilton Hotel-Lobby at night.jpg` goes to
  *Hilton Hotel* with the suggested Title *Lobby at night*.
- Capitals, accents and underscores don't matter.
- Click **Download the exact names** to get a list to send to whoever is
  collecting the photos.

Fill in **Source** (Individual or AI Agent), **Supplied by** (who sent
them) and a **Date**. The date is used for photos that don't record when
they were taken.

## Curating

Nothing goes into an album until it's curated. Each photo shows as:

- **Ready**: matched by its exact name.
- **Check match**: the name was nearly right. Confirm or change the
  hotel or restaurant.
- **No match**: choose the hotel or restaurant yourself. If several
  share the same name, you choose which one.
- **Duplicate**: the same photo is already in the album, or appears
  twice in the upload. It's left unticked.

For each photo, set the **Title**, **Description**, **Date** and **Sort
order**, and tick **Add to album**. Then click **Add ticked to albums**.
**Save** keeps your edits so you can come back later. **Leave out
unticked** and **Discard upload** drop what you don't want.

## Browsing the album

- **Album** view: a slider with the photo's Title, Description and Date,
  and a strip of thumbnails. The left and right arrow keys move between
  photos; click a photo to open it full size.
- **List** view: edit Title, Description, Date and Sort order in place,
  then **Save changes**. **Number in the order shown** sets the Sort
  order 1, 2, 3 … for the current order. Tick photos and click **Delete
  ticked** to remove them.
- **Search** finds Title, Description or Date (for example `2026-05`).
  **From** and **To** limit the dates, and **Order** sorts by album
  order, date or title.

An **AI Agent** that collects photos from the web will feed the same
curation step. Its photos show where they were found.
