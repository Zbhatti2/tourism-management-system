**AI Usage** (a Tenant Admin page, opened from **AI Agents**) shows what
your organisation's AI agents have cost. Every call to the AI model is
recorded with:

- what it was for, such as the Image Collector for a particular hotel or
  an Agent Run;
- who started it;
- the model used and the tokens it read and wrote;
- any web searches;
- the cost.

Use the month picker to see earlier months. These charges are billed to
your organisation.

**Monthly allowance:** the TMS administrator can set a monthly AI
allowance for your organisation. Once it's used up, new agent runs are
refused until the next month, and an agent that's already running stops
before its next hotel. The bar shows how much of this month's allowance
is used.
