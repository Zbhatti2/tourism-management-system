Intelligence (under **Organization Intelligence**, alongside Documents)
is a running, append-only journal of freeform operational knowledge —
things staff pick up in the field that don't belong on any one
Organization, Supplier, or POI record. Examples: two spellings of the
same place meaning the same thing, a hotel's pet or childcare policy, or
a route's drive time in bad weather.

## How it's different from editing a record

There's no history or versioning here, and no editing an old entry to
"correct" it. Superseding outdated knowledge is done by simply adding a
newer-dated entry about the same topic — **Entry date** and **Entered by**
together establish which entry is current. The list sorts newest entry
date first, so the most current information on any topic naturally
surfaces at the top without anything needing to reference or replace a
prior entry.
