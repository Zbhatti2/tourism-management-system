Organizations are companies, government bodies, and other institutions —
a governing authority for a Point of Interest, a partner agency, or any
other organization you deal with that isn't itself a Supplier in your
catalog.

## What's on an organization record

- **Core details** — organization name, type, and notes.
- **Addresses** — one or more, with the same Region → Country →
  Province/State → City cascade used everywhere else in TMS.
- **Contact info** — emails, phones, and Reference Links (URL/document
  path + description), plus the ability to attach existing Contacts as
  this organization's people.
- **Organization Intelligence** — an organization can be the subject of
  entries in the tenant-wide Documents and Intelligence modules (see
  those sections of this help panel).

## Organizations vs. Suppliers

An Organization is not automatically a vendor you book through — that's
what the separate Suppliers module is for. A Supplier can reference an
Organization (for example, a hotel's parent company), but the two lists
are independent.
