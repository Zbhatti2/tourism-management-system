Many of your Points of Interest and Suppliers come from the **Platform
Catalog**, a shared list of places, hotels and restaurants that TMS
maintains for all tour operators. They're marked with a small blue
cloud icon in your lists.

These records stay up to date automatically. When the catalog corrects
a phone number or adds a website, your copy changes too, as long as
you haven't changed that field yourself.

## When you've changed a field

If you edited a field and the catalog later has a *different* value
for it, nothing is overwritten. Instead the record shows **Platform has
a different value**, and **Platform updates** appears at the top of the
Points of Interest and Suppliers lists with a count.

For each field, choose:

- **Accept** to take the catalog's value. That field follows the catalog
  again from then on.
- **Keep mine** to keep yours. You won't be asked about that value
  again, only if the catalog changes it once more.

## From the Platform Catalog

Each record that came from the catalog has a **From the Platform
Catalog** section. Open it to see the catalog's extra details, such as
entry fees, opening hours, star ratings, cuisine and
images. These details always show the latest catalog information.
Your own notes, contacts, rooms, prices and documents are yours alone
and are never changed by the catalog.
